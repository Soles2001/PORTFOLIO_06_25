Cofi

Brand Identity for Cofi

COFI · 2024 · Branding · Packaging

Cofi celebrates origin and taste by turning coffee attributes into a living identity. A modular code maps balance, acidity, and body, making selection transparent for both newcomers and aficionados. With packaging colors drawn from each bean's place of origin, the system feels both educational and emotive. It shows how data, storytelling, and design can democratize specialty coffee.
