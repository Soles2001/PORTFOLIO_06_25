Minority

APP Design for Minority

Minority · 2025 · Product Design · App UI

Minority is a mobile app that spotlights lesser-known sports and makes them accessible to everyone. It connects people with nearby clubs to promote inclusion, diversity, and physical activity. With a vibrant, intuitive interface, users can discover and filter sports by interest, ability, or proximity. A Laus Awards finalist, it shows how design can drive equality, and social impact.
