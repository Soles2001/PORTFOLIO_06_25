Valencia Wines

Wine Packaging for Valencia Wines

Valencia Wines · 2024 · Packaging · Illustration

A graphic language that toasts Valencia's character, from the color and ceremony of Las Fallas to contemporary elegance. Labels and collateral balance festive forms with refined typography to honor terroir without cliche. With a flexible palette and clear hierarchy, the system travels from tasting rooms to export shelves. It shows how regional codes can feel premium, current, and unmistakably local.
