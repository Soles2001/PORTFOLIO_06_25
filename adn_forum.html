Design Language for ADN Forum

ADN Forum · 2024 · Editorial · Event Branding

A poster-led identity for a forum on design and new media that invites participation over spectacle. Variable typographic structures adapt to speakers, venues, and formats while keeping the message crystal clear. With a modular grid and responsive templates, the system shifts seamlessly from print to screens. It shows how identity can be a platform for ideas, not just a frame.
